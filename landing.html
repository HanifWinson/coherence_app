OCT triage for optometry practices

Only open the scans that need a human.

Coherence sorts every OCT scan into cleared, rescan or review — so your optometrists spend their time on the scans that actually need them, and bad captures get retaken while the patient is still in the chair.

Three outcomes, decided by where the AI is unsure

Most tools tell you what they detected. Coherence tells you how sure it is — and the shape of that uncertainty decides what happens next.

Cleared

The model agrees with itself across all its passes.

→ no review · routine recall

Rescan

Uncertainty is spread across the whole image — a capture problem, not a clinical one.

→ retake before the patient leaves

Review

The capture is fine, but one region is genuinely ambiguous.

→ optometrist opens it; a heatmap marks where to look

Patient details never leave your practice

  • Scans are de-identified in your browser, before anything is uploaded — names, IDs and dates of birth are stripped, and names burned into the image are masked.
  • Each scan gets a random case ID. Your practice keeps the mapping; we never receive it.
  • Scan images are deleted once the report is generated. We keep derived numbers only.
  • We do not use your uploads to train our models.
